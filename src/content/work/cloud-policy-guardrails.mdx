At Cognira, I built a **pre-deployment Policy-as-Code guardrail** for **Terraform-based Azure infrastructure**. The goal was to make risky configurations visible and actionable during the review process, rather than after deployment.

![Conceptual diagram of Terraform plan checks through Conftest, Rego, Python reports and CI](/images/cognira-guardrails.png)

*Conceptual policy validation workflow; no internal policy source code is published here.*

## 1. Policy evaluation of Terraform plans

I developed **approximately 50 custom Rego policies** with **deny/warn** decisions, spanning **15 governance domains**. These included access control, network exposure, sensitive-resource protection and cost governance.

The workflow converts proposed infrastructure changes to **Terraform plan JSON** and uses **Conftest/OPA** to evaluate the plan against the Rego policies **before deployment**.

## 2. Python reporting and reviewer decisions

I implemented a **Python engine** that transforms the Conftest JSON findings into readable **terminal, Markdown and GitHub Actions reports**.

Findings are linked to their affected resources and summarized into four decision states:

- **PASSED:** checks can proceed under the configured rules.
- **REVIEW REQUIRED:** warnings or findings need human evaluation.
- **BLOCKED:** enforcing conditions prevent continuing.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** the evaluation cannot confidently produce a conclusive decision.

These labels are summaries of the implemented decision engine, not invented compliance certifications.

## 3. CI/CD integration: audit or enforcement

I integrated validation into a **GitHub Actions Terraform pipeline**, publishing findings automatically and supporting two execution modes:

- **Audit mode:** report the issues without blocking the workflow.
- **Strict enforcement:** fail the pipeline and prevent `terraform apply` while active policy violations remain.

## 4. Auditable time-limited exceptions

I designed an exception mechanism with **ticket references**, **exact policy/resource/severity matching**, and **automatic expiry checks**. Approved and expired exceptions remain visible in reports, maintaining traceability instead of silently suppressing findings.

## Engineering outcomes

This work connected **cloud security policy**, **developer feedback**, and **CI/CD enforcement** around a consistent decision workflow. It demonstrates pre-deployment governance and DevSecOps automation, without implying penetration testing or external compliance certification.
